# Integrating Sustainability into Higher Education - CDE, University of Bern

This platform is hosted by the [Centre for Development and Environment (CDE)](https://www.cde.unibe.ch/), a leading research centre for sustainable development. CDE combines research, education, and practice to co-create knowledge for just and sustainable transformations worldwide.

## 🎓 About the course

*Integrating Sustainability into Higher Education: Theories, Concepts, and Practical Strategies* is a co-created, openly licensed course for higher education lecturers who want to integrate sustainable development (SD) into their teaching. It was developed as a tri-national collaboration between CDE (University of Bern), the University of Nairobi, and Addis Ababa University, and piloted online with Kenyan faculty (2024) and on-site in Ethiopia (2025).

The course is structured into four sessions, each building on the previous one through a cumulative individual project — the ESD Action Plan:

- **Session 01 — Introduction to Sustainable Development.** Foundational concepts of SD, its historical development, and the global challenges it responds to.
- **Session 02 — Interlinkages of Sustainability Dimensions and Role of Science & Higher Education.** How the social, economic, and environmental dimensions interlink, and the role of science, higher education, and individual disciplines.
- **Session 03 — Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) & Constructive Alignment.** ESD as a pedagogical approach, and constructive alignment as the course-design tool that translates it into practice.
- **Session 04 — Presentation of Action Plans.** Participants present the action plans they developed for integrating (E)SD into their own teaching.

## 🎯 Learning outcomes

Upon completion of this course, participants will:

- Have an in-depth understanding of key SD concepts and theories.
- Be able to establish thematic connections between sustainable development and their own field.
- Understand the importance of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) for sustainable futures.
- Be able to design a constructively aligned course unit that integrates (E)SD into their own teaching.

## 👥 Target group

The course is aimed at higher education teaching staff from all disciplines. Decision-makers and academic programme directors may also find it valuable to attend.

**Online course site:** Please find the course [here](https://cdeteaching.github.io/IntegrateSustainabilityIntoHigherEducation_pilotcourse/)

## 🌍 Adapt & reuse

This course is designed to be adopted and adapted by higher education institutions worldwide — as a whole, session by session, or with your own local case studies swapped in. See the [Adapt & Reuse guide](https://cdeteaching.github.io/IntegrateSustainabilityIntoHigherEducation_pilotcourse/adapt.html) on the course site.
