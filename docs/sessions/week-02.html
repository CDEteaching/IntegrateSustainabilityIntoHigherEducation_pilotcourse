Session 02: Interlinkages of Sustainability Dimensions and Role of Science & Higher Education

Session 02 Self-paced online · 90-100 min Plenary

Interlinkages of Sustainability Dimensions and Role of Science & Higher Education

This session moves from the three sustainability dimensions treated separately in Session 01 to how they interlink in practice, and introduces the role of science, higher education, and individual disciplines, including participants’ own, in advancing sustainable development.

Session Overview

Format Self-paced online module (adaptable for live/synchronous delivery)
Duration Approx. 90-100 min self-paced (equivalent to a 2h live session)
Preparation for participants Completion of Session 1, including the “Review of course content” task

Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, participants will be able to:

  1. Examine the interlinkages between the social, economic, and environmental dimensions of sustainable development.
  2. Define the role of science and higher education in addressing sustainability challenges, including their own role as lecturers.
  3. Reflect on the potential contributions of their own discipline to sustainable development.

Session Outline

1. Interlinkages of Sustainability Dimensions (25 min)
Input, example, and group work.
2. Role of Science and Higher Education (20 min)
Input and examples.

3. Reflecting on Disciplinary Contributions (5 min)

4. Discussion/reflection (10 min)
The role of higher education at participants’ own university.

5. Links between Disciplines and Sustainable Development (15 min)

6. Key messages and FAQ (5 min)

7. Task after Session 2 (10 min)
Identify opportunities to incorporate SD into your course.

Preparation tasks & learning activities

Before the session

Submit or review the Task after Session 1 (review of course content).

During the session

  • Complete the group/individual exercise mapping the three dimensions onto a chosen sustainability challenge.
  • Complete the discussion/reflection on the role of higher education at your own institution, and reflect on your own role.
  • Complete the discipline-SD linkage mapping task.

After the session

Task after Session 2, identify opportunities to incorporate SD into your course: consider the SDGs (and other frameworks, according to your context), global sustainability challenges, or different sustainability perspectives, and articulate what links your own discipline has with sustainable development.

Recommendation for facilitation

  • For the “role of lecturer/educator” exercise, use a live polling tool (e.g. Mentimeter) if run synchronously; if used as a self-paced delivery, replace it with a structured written reflection using the same prompt.
  • The Ethiopia/Kenya case studies and the Bern/Addis Ababa/Nairobi institutional examples are illustrations, not a fixed reference set. Adapt to your local context and/or actively invite participants to substitute examples from their own country and institution.
