Who we are

About this course

This course was developed in 2023 by a tri-national (Swiss, Kenyan, and Ethiopian) project team of nine members, representing:

  • University of Bern, Switzerland (Centre for Development and Environment)
  • Addis Ababa University, Ethiopia (Water and Land Resource Centre, and College of Education and Behavioral Studies)
  • University of Nairobi, Kenya (Department of Geography, Population and Environmental Studies, and the Centre for Training and Integrated Research on ASAL Development)

The team iteratively built and revised the course’s four sessions through collaborative working groups. The course was first piloted online with higher education faculty in Kenya in 2024, and on-site in Ethiopia in 2025. Following revision based on both pilots, it is now available for wider adoption.

Course objective

The course is designed to empower higher education lecturers in Ethiopia and Kenya with the knowledge, tools, and strategies to effectively integrate sustainable development (SD) and SD concepts and theories into their teaching. It provides an in-depth understanding of key SD concepts and theories, guides participants in establishing thematic connections between sustainable development and their own field, and highlights the importance of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) for sustainable futures.

Target group

The course is aimed at higher education teaching staff from all disciplines. Decision-makers and academic programme directors may also find it valuable to attend.

How you can use this course

There are several ways to engage with this material, depending on your needs:

  • Work through the course independently, at your own pace: All four sessions, materials, and exercises are freely available for self-paced learning — use them as they are, or draw on individual sections and materials for your own purposes.
  • Adapt and deliver the course at your own institution: The sessions, examples, and exercises can be localized to your own context — the illustrative cases used throughout (from Switzerland, Ethiopia, and Kenya) are meant as models, not fixed content, and are designed to be substituted with examples relevant to your own setting.
  • Co-design a course with us: If you are interested in developing a tailored version of this course together with the original project team, we welcome the opportunity to collaborate — please get in touch to discuss what this could look like [contact details to be confirmed].

Session overview

Course Sessions

The course consists of four sessions, each building on the previous one through a cumulative, individual project — the ESD Action Plan. Across the four sessions, participants develop their own ESD Action Plan: a concrete, ready-to-use lesson or session that integrates sustainable development into their own teaching, grounded in their own discipline and institutional context.

Foundations

Open Educational Resource

Adapt This Course

This course is designed to be adopted and adapted by Higher Education Institutions worldwide. Materials are modular — individual sessions can be integrated without running the full course. Each session includes a recommendations for facilitation.
